## 2026-11-18 v3.37 scratched players come off the card and their slot is refilled (asked for by Armanddo; lineup news rule and pick policy, no page change)
- What went wrong: Scottie Barnes was ruled out about 2:30 PM on Nov 18. ESPN had him Out at both evening runs (5:07 and 6:17 PM) and both lineups runs, and the Games tab showed it. But the lineup news treated any row with a player's name in the lines file as proof he was playing, so one leftover row kept him in the model's lineup all night. His Over 5.5 assists ticket stayed open, the evening pass saw three tickets and no room, and the 21 and 24 sides that cleared the bar those two runs had nowhere to go. No book in the evening pull had a two-sided assists line on him by then (his ticket got no closing number).
- Fix 1, lineup news (model input): quotes overrule a listed Out only when three sportsbooks still post his main market with both prices. Alternate ladders, exchanges and one-sided rows no longer count. On the late runs (lineups, close), where the lines on file are older than the injury report, a plain Out always stands. The run log now names anyone kept out despite a leftover quote and which books had him.
- Fix 2, scratches (pick policy): on evening, lineups and close runs, an open ticket on a player listed Out today (or on the NBA inactive list) is voided before the game, the same result grading would give it the next morning. The ticket keeps its numbers; it gains a scratch note and its case file says when and why.
- Fix 3, the slot is refilled (pick policy): a scratched ticket gives back its slot, its per-game count and its units. On the evening run, which has fresh prices, the next side that clears every test takes it, under the same rules as any evening pick, and its case file says whose slot it took. Late runs scratch but do not replace: they have no fresh prices, and a paper bet at a price nobody could still get would flatter the record.
- Safety: only a plain Out scratches a ticket (never doubtful, questionable or injured reserve). If a scratched player plays after all, grading puts the ticket back and settles it on the result.
- Replay of Nov 18 with the midday books: Barnes scratched, Immanuel Quickley Over 5.5 assists +120 logged in his place, the day at 5.0 units, every earlier bet byte for byte the same. Old rule against the same test file: Barnes treated as playing. In the real run the model would also have re-projected Toronto without Barnes first, so the replacement could differ.
- Evidence: this fixes a defect, it does not tune a threshold, and the audit cannot grade a void, so there is no backtest behind `news_books` = 3. Watch the "kept out despite a leftover quote" and "listed out but quoted by 3+ books" log lines for a few weeks; if a player who really was back gets held out, lower it to 2.
- Tonight's Barnes ticket is not edited by hand. Tomorrow's morning grade voids it as before.

## 2026-11-18 v3.20 the Sim tab: every game played 10,000 times (asked for by Armanddo; display and tracking only)
- New pipeline files: simcore.py (the simulator), sim.py (runs the slate, logs and grades), simtab.js and simtab.css (the tab, poured into the page by build.py), sim_bt.json (the test numbers the page shows), and the research tools sim_prep_nba.py, sim_prep_nfl.py, sim_bt.py, sim_bt_nfl.py, sim_joint.py, sim_joint_nfl.py. run.sh runs `sim.py grade` then `sim.py` after games.py; site.py copies research/sim_nba.json and sim_nfl.json. - What it does. Each run draws the game first (final margin and total around the books' spread and total), then the box score from the top down so every number adds up. NBA: minutes move with how lopsided the run is (a 36-minute star plays about 13% less in a 30-point game, the end of the bench about 30% more; table fitted on 2024-25), team threes, rebounds and assists follow the team's points, and the team's numbers are dealt to the players by their projections (Dirichlet-multinomial). A player's points are 3 x his threes plus his share of the rest, so the team score is the players' points. NFL: a side that ends up behind its spread throws more and runs less (fitted on 2023-24: pass attempts -0.46% and carries +1.07% per point), targets and carries are dealt by projected share, yards are drawn catch by catch and carry by carry, scaled by how well the offense played in that run, and the quarterback's yards are his receivers' yards.
- Player numbers are the desk's own, not the raw model: centred on the slate and blended with the books per market exactly as daily.py prices (mean = market + w x (c x model - market)). NBA only: players with a points line keep that number and the rest of the rotation shares what is left of the books' team total, because the sum of every rotation player's number runs well over the team total. ## 2026-11-17 v3.6 confident plays, a second look at the projections, NBA matchup sheets (policy change, asked for by Armanddo; pick policy VER 1.5)
- What the audit found. The model on its own is sound against history (on 25,000 test games it beats each player's last-five average in every stat with no overall lean, and its chances for 70% to 90% lines come true 73% to 94% of the time). Live, it is less accurate than the books: on the 300 graded sides from Nov 16 the books' implied numbers missed points by 4.7 a game and the model by 5.2. Centring puts the model's slate average on the market's, market by market, so a general lean cannot create bets. w is relearned each run from the audit (tonight: NBA pts 0.29, reb 0.46, ast 0.41; NFL rec 0.29, rush 0.11, pass 0.23), pulled toward 0.25.
