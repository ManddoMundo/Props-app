## 2026-11-17 v3.6 confident plays, a second look at the projections, NBA matchup sheets (policy change, asked for by Armanddo; pick policy VER 1.5)
- What the audit found. The model on its own is sound against history (on 25,000 test games it beats each player's last-five average in every stat with no overall lean, and its chances for 70% to 90% lines come true 73% to 94% of the time). Live, it is less accurate than the books: on the 300 graded sides from Nov 16 the books' implied numbers missed points by 4.7 a game and the model by 5.2. Before v1.3 the desk trusted the model alone and was overconfident: sides it called 65% to 80% won about half the time. Since Nov 16 it blends the model with seven books and learns how far to trust the model per market (points: 16% model, 84% books), which is why few edge plays clear the bar. Today the model runs about 4% under the books on points; the per-slate centring takes that out before any bet.
- The Board now shows the desk number (the model moved onto the books, the number the odds use) as the projection whenever a book line is on file; hovering the cell gives the model alone and the books. Without a line it says Model.
- New lane: confident plays (daily.py confident, run after pick in the midday job). An outcome the other books rate at least 72% likely, main line or alternate line, that the model also rates at least 68% on its own, at a Hard Rock price no worse than the books' fair chance by at least a point. Heavy prices are allowed (-140 to -500); overpaying is not. Overs lose 2 points of chance for an early exit, and no chance above 93% is trusted. Same lineup tests as the edge lane plus 24 projected minutes for NBA and 3 other books on the main line. Up to 2 a league a day, one a game, never a player the edge lane bet.
