## 2026-11-20 v3.51 the retest: open tickets are tested again at the later prices (asked for by Armanddo: "1 only"; tracking and display, no model or pick policy change)
- Why: the later runs repriced the whole board but never went back to a ticket already made. A pick whose edge had gone by 6 PM looked the same on the page as one that still stood.
- daily.py, retest(): on the evening pass (about 6 PM) and on a lineups run with a fresh price pull (about 6:40 and 9:15 PM), every edge ticket and lean still open for today is put through the pick test again at the line and price on the board now. The verdict is saved on the ticket as `retest`: still clears, would not be picked now (with the test it fails, in the same words the pass list uses), or no line to test against. It also records the desk's chance and edge now on the ticket as held, at its own line and price.
- What it never does: change a ticket's line, odds, stake or status, add a ticket, void a ticket, or feed the pick rules. Midday tickets stand as made, as before. A lean is retested under the lean bar. Confident plays, alternate lines and anytime touchdowns are not retested (they are not picked by this test). A scratched ticket is skipped.
- Each later pass writes over the last verdict; the first one is kept beside it, so a ticket that cleared at 6 PM and failed at 6:40 says so.
- The report gains a line once retested tickets are graded: record, units and later line value for tickets that still cleared against those that did not. That is how we learn whether a failed retest means anything.
- DISPLAY, index.src.html: an open ticket with a retest shows a strip under its numbers (green: still clears; red: would not be picked now, with the reason; dashed: no line). The case file gets a Retest block with the new line and price, the desk's chance at them, and the ticket as held.
- Tested: replayed on Nov 18's tickets against that day's evening and late prices (all three still cleared; nothing saved), and on made-up sides for a lean, a thin edge, an edge past the ceiling, a questionable player, a confident play and a missing line. ## 2026-11-20 v3.50 NBA points and threes are built from shots, not from recent points (asked for by Armanddo: "added to the actual Fair Line app ... as accurate as possible"; MODEL change, plus a display change listed apart below)
- Why: a prop video argued that a stat should be projected from what sits under it (volume, then quality, then conversion), and that a run of good shooting is mostly luck. Tested on our own model before changing anything.
- MODEL, nba.py: the per-minute rate for points is now two-point tries, three-point tries and free throw tries per minute (15-game memory, as before) times a steadied percentage for each (pulled toward the position's with a prior of 60 twos, 120 threes, 40 free throws). The rate for threes is three-point tries per minute times a three-point percentage with a 100-game memory and a 240-attempt prior. ## 2026-11-16 v1.3 market check, lineup news, lean fix (model and policy change, asked for by Armanddo)
Why: 1,138 graded sides showed the v1.2 model was not beating the book. Hit rate sat near 50 to 55% at every edge level, and it had a lean: it called Unders 55.6% likely and they hit 49.2%. 22 of 25 desk bets were Unders.
- Lineup news (new `daily.py news`, read by nba.py and nfl.py): ESPN injury report plus the books. Listed out sits, quoted by a book plays, questionable is priced but never picked, the book's passing yards market names the NFL quarterback (a quarterback new to the team is brought in with his own history).
- NBA rotation fill (ROT_T = 250): the live lineup was everyone in the last box score, sometimes 12 to 14 players, which squeezed starters' minutes. Now filled by minutes to a 250 team total. Walk-forward test over 2,978 team games, players projected 20+ minutes: minutes bias +0.20 to 0.00 (2025-26) and +0.13 to -0.04 (2026-27); points avg miss 5.163 to 5.154 and 5.216 to 5.182; minutes avg miss 4.64 to 4.59 and 4.64 to 4.57.
- Market check: odds are pulled from Hard Rock plus DraftKings, FanDuel and BetMGM at the same credit cost. Each book's line is turned into the projection it implies; the median of the other three is the market view.
- Pricing mean = market + w x (centred model - market). Centring puts the model's slate average on the market's, market by market, so a general lean cannot create bets. w is relearned each run from the audit (tonight: NBA pts 0.29, reb 0.46, ast 0.41; NFL rec 0.29, rush 0.11, pass 0.23), pulled toward 0.25.
